# Code of conduct

PalHarbor's community should be welcoming, constructive, and safe.

Participants must:

- Treat people with respect regardless of identity, background, experience, or
  point of view.
- Give technical feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Respect privacy and never publish credentials, player information, or private
  vulnerability details.
- Stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as disruptive or unsafe.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, close contributions, temporarily restrict
participation, or permanently ban participants when necessary. Reports will be
handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.
